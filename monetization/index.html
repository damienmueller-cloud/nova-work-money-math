Monetization & disclosures

Work & Money Math is free to use. This page is the disclosure home for how the sitemay be funded later — not a claim that ads or affiliates are live today.

Current status (honest)

  • Core calculators are free and run in your browser.
  • No advertising network is enabled on this build unless this page and the footer say otherwise.
  • No affiliate links are active unless labelled as such on the page that contains them.
  • Calculator inputs stay on your device for the math; we do not sell them.

When support may appear

We only consider ads or affiliates after meaningful organic traffic (roughly sustained ~1,000+ sessions per month or a clear growing trend). Until then, the site stays free tools only (Stage 0).

Possible future support

  1. Privacy-conscious ads — e.g. Google AdSense or a developer-friendly network such as Carbon Ads. If enabled, we will name the provider here and update theprivacy page.
  2. Affiliate links — tools freelancers already search for (invoicing such as FreshBooks or Wave, business banking / accounts, Notion or similar productivity). Paid referrals will be disclosed next to the link — never as fake “recommended” without a clear label.
  3. Optional Pro unlock — one-time extras (for example CSV export or saved scenarios), never a paywall on the core math.

Placement rules (if ads or affiliates go live)

  • Non-intrusive: after a calculator result or below the fold — not covering inputs or results.
  • No interstitial or fake-close patterns.
  • Affiliate copy will say when a link may earn a commission.
  • No cross-promotion of unrelated brands on this site.

What we will not do

  • Fake “sponsored” badges or pretend ads are live when they are not.
  • Sell calculator inputs.
  • Claim tax, financial, or legal advice — take-home tools stay illustrative estimates.
