About

Work & Money Math is a free calculator hub for freelancers, contractors, and side hustlers. It helps with everyday money questions: day rates, project fees, platform cuts, runway, and rough take-home toys.

Everything runs in your browser. There are no accounts and no paid APIs in this MVP. Inputs stay on your device for the calculation — we do not need them to serve the page.

Important labels

  • Tax and take-home tools are illustrative estimates, not advice.
  • Laws, GST/VAT, and employment rules differ by country and situation.
  • For real decisions, talk to a qualified professional.

Roadmap (honest)

The site may later include privacy-friendly analytics, ads, or affiliate links so it can stay free. Any paid “Pro” features would be optional extras (for example exports), not a paywall on the core math.
