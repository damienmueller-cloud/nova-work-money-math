Hours freed by a rate raise

How many weekly hours you can drop after raising your rate and keep the same income.

Hours freed per week
—

    How it works

    oldHours = goal ÷ oldRate; newHours = goal ÷ newRate; freed = old − new. Raising rates is often the cleanest way to buy back time without cutting income.
